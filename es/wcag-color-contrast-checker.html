Online color contrast ratio tester for comprobador contraste wcag. Evaluate WCAG 2.1 AA and AAA standards to ensure digital products are accessible to people with vision impairments.

Instrucciones Paso a Paso

  1. Select or type the text foreground color and background color in HEX.
  2. Check the calculated contrast ratio (e.g. 4.5:1 for standard web text).
  3. Verify pass/fail badges across normal text, headlines, and UI elements.

Preguntas Frecuentes y Referencia CSS

What is the minimum WCAG AA ratio for normal text?

WCAG 2.1 Level AA requires a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 for normal text (under 18pt) and at least 3:1 for large text (18pt or 14pt bold).
